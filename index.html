Start with 4 lifting days. The fifth day is optional and should never compromise recovery.

Shoulder rule: clearance to exercise does not automatically mean every heavy barbell position is appropriate. Surgeon/physio restrictions override this plan. Stop and get guidance for sharp pain, catching, instability, new weakness, or symptoms that persist/worsen after training.

Workouts

Weeks 1–4: technique first, RPE 5–6. Weeks 5–8: build, RPE 6–7. Weeks 9–12: strength introduction, mostly RPE 6–8. Avoid failure.

Compound lift roadmap

Squat

Goblet squat → box/safety-bar squat → barbell squat only when shoulder positioning is comfortable and cleared.

Hinge / Deadlift

Hip-hinge drill → elevated kettlebell deadlift → trap-bar deadlift → RDL/conventional variations as technique develops.

Press

Neutral-grip machine press → neutral-grip dumbbell press → barbell bench only after the repaired shoulder tolerates the range and load.

Pull

Chest-supported row → cable row/pulldown → progressively heavier unsupported pulling as appropriate.

Carry

Light farmer carry → heavier carries as grip and shoulder tolerance permit.

How to progress

Use a rep range. When every set reaches the top of the range with clean technique and ~2–3 reps still in reserve, add the smallest practical amount of weight next session. Upper-body progression should be more conservative than lower-body progression during shoulder recovery.
